# Product

Snooze Pleaase is an Indian premium nightwear brand: hand-picked cotton nightsuits, co-ord sets, robes and towels for kids, plus Mom & Me loungewear. Tagline: "Sleep naturally, dream sustainably."

## Visitor
Parents (mostly on phones, in India) buying for a child's bedtime. They need size, fabric, price, stock and delivery clarity fast, and they should feel the brand's warmth.

## Mode
Persuade on Home and Shop, Operate on product, cart and checkout, Read on policy pages.

## Brand commitments
- The royal-blue "Dream Big, Sleep Cozy" mockup is the pinned visual world: kraft gift boxes, crayon-style moon, clouds and stars, yellow and pink pill buttons.
- Blue on Home and Shop; warm cream paper on product, checkout, forms and policy pages.
- Currency INR. Prices come from the catalog; offers and shipping rules live in `src/lib/config.js`.
- Never invent reviews, customer counts or certifications.

## Decisions taken with the owner
React + Vite; storefront only; mocked payment; 10% auto discount, free shipping over ₹1,999, COD/UPI/card; "Mom & Me" in the main nav; doodles redrawn as SVG.
